1) Unzip Build folder
2) double click "StorageOrganizer" file to start
3) screen resolution should be 1920x1080. if using widescreen to view, it is recommended to change the resolution of your screen to 1920x1080 in Settings->Display
4) click the "Generate Test Boxes" to explore program functionality
5) if you cant close the program due to the quit button being out of reach, hit Control+Alt+Delete and look for StorageOrganizer in the Apps list, right click and press End Task
6) you are able to place boxes in non official "Lane" spots, but they cannot currently be stacked.
